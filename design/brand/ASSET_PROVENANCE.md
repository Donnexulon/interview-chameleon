# Interview Chameleon brand asset provenance

Recorded on 2026-09-12 for the 1.0.0-beta.1 release.

## Approved direction

`interview-chameleon-approved-brand-board.png` is the product owner's selected brand direction. It was supplied directly by the product owner after they generated and compared several concepts. It establishes the chameleon/conversation symbol, editorial wordmark, and the near-black, warm-ivory, and burnt-orange palette.

The project does not assert that this record replaces trademark clearance or legal review. Those remain human release gates.

## Ollama setup illustration

`static/assets/setup/ollama-llama.png` is cropped directly from the first-run
setup design board supplied and selected by the product owner. It preserves the
board's hand-drawn llama, orange alert badge, and ground shadow and is shown only
on the Ollama installation/start step. The Interview Chameleon mark remains the
application identity in the window header and elsewhere.

## Production symbol

`static/assets/brand/interview-chameleon-mark.png` was generated from the approved board with OpenAI image generation, then copied without manual redrawing. It has a transparent background and is used inside the product.

Final prompt:

> Create one clean, production-ready, symbol-only logo asset based strictly on the approved Interview Chameleon mark in the supplied brand identity sheet. Preserve the same distinctive geometry and concept: a compact near-black chameleon silhouette in profile, curled circular tail that reads subtly as a letter C and contains a small conversation bubble with exactly three burnt-orange dots, a single burnt-orange sweep along the chameleon's back, and the ivory/black circular eye. Center the mark in a square canvas with generous even padding. Use a genuinely transparent background with preserved alpha. Crisp flat vector-like edges and strong silhouette, optimized to remain recognizable at 16 px and polished at 512 px. No wordmark, no letters, no tagline, no labels, no border, no rounded-square app tile, no mockup, no shadows, no paper texture, no gradients, no additional objects. Do not redesign the chosen mark or add decorative detail; isolate and refine it faithfully.

## Windows app icon

`static/assets/brand/interview-chameleon-app-icon.png` was generated from the same approved board with OpenAI image generation. `desktop/interview-chameleon.ico` is a multi-resolution ICO conversion of that PNG and is embedded in the Windows executable and installer.

Final prompt:

> Create one production-ready Windows app icon based strictly on the approved “APP ICON (SYMBOL ONLY)” shown in the supplied Interview Chameleon brand identity sheet. Square 1024 × 1024 composition. Preserve the same rounded near-black square tile; centered ivory/white chameleon in profile; curled circular tail containing a small near-black conversation bubble with exactly three burnt-orange dots; one burnt-orange sweep along the chameleon's back; and the simple ivory/black eye. Use generous optical padding and a strong, simplified silhouette that remains unmistakable at 16 px. The outer canvas beyond the rounded tile must be genuinely transparent with preserved alpha. Flat vector-like colors and crisp edges. No wordmark, no letters, no tagline, no labels, no border around the canvas, no mockup, no shadow, no texture, no gradients, and no extra objects. Do not redesign the selected mark.
